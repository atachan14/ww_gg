## Priority clarification: the auxiliary ledger is not algorithmically required

The user's clarification supersedes treating repeated ownership/frame checks as a permanent runtime requirement. Same-group semantics and Q/P updates are necessary; a registry repeatedly proving that an internal result still belongs to its engine is not. This increment only consolidates that registry and cleans its lifetime; it does **not** yet implement a closed calculation API or eliminate the ledger. Its runtime acceleration remains unadopted.

The code already has the right foundation: GameData and BoardData in board_flow.py are frozen, and GameRuntime owns immutable common settings and creates a new runtime for a new setup. SavedCutOutGraph.data/engine and InformationSetForecast.rules/strategy plus public cache dictionaries remain replaceable in the experimental interface. The ledger's _owner_current, _groups_record, frame comparisons and issued-generation/cache-ticket maps largely compensate for that experimental openness, rather than being prerequisites of the probability algorithm.

Next structural priority is to reuse those immutable snapshots and calculation-local cache ownership. Open one calculation from a saved BoardData plus fixed strategy/view inputs; keep its engine/caches internal; create child frames only through the existing common acquisition/filter/death/report constructors. A Board edit/recalculation opens a new calculation instead of replacing the data behind an existing internal frame. The API should not separately accept arbitrary laws/groups or foreign raw cache entries and then try to certify them. Unsupported/general legacy inputs keep the ordinary entry. Once that boundary is real, owner/configuration checks belong at the external entry, and independent group invariance/full-history/cold assertions belong in development tests, not after every internally generated child.

Removable auxiliary work then includes receipt issuance/weak registries, repeated owner/frame identity checks, generation and policy stamps, cache-proof tickets, and duplicated proof-only copies of branch weights. Preserve the actual algorithms: initial lawful joint-history generation, legal-target policy, owner-only observation filtering, normalization and event/branch/choice masses, separate public Q and actual P propagation, named group splitting/transport needed by orbit evaluation, and SavedOrigin recipes for required cold readback. Root user-input validation, zero-support errors and numerical memoization remain at their actual semantic boundaries. Do not simply switch off checks on the current mutable experimental API, nor invent a second solver or a new layer of certification objects. First close ownership by construction, then measure the same bounded tasks before claiming a benefit or extending cases.

At an initial group-model snapshot, permutations inside equal claim classes preserve the generated initial assignment/history law. Fix every named endpoint in public ability reports. Q and P remain separate. Own observation conditions P while leaving public Q unchanged. Reporting uses the existing complete-history public generation and private likelihood transfer. For wolf information cells I, actual mass is w_I=sum(P over I), and the configured target policy q_I(t) is calculated from public Q conditioned on wolf-owned I, never the viewer's secrets. ## Report-integral mathematical design (development only)

This checkpoint changes no production module, policy, API, limit or admission. ### Existing equation and the three different measures

Start AFTER the common acquisition, owner-observation validation and physical/public continuation projection, at a pre-publication morning. Let h contain complete roles/acquisitions/guard/attack records, Q(h) be the current public joint law, and P(u) the separate normalized actual/viewer law. The projection pi(h)=(roles, acquisitions) is exactly the identity used by information_weights.observer_report_weights; the viewer's guard/attack metadata is preserved, not required to be identical to a public history. Write Qpi(z)=sum_{pi(h)=z} Q(h). This Q is the already established Model-A public continuation law, not a posterior obtained by replacing it with P or with a physical branch mass.

Let m be a complete medium-report vector. The existing uniform fake-medium instruction law and truthful medium reports define L_m(roles), with sum_m L_m(roles)=1. Its public probability and conditional history law are

\[
 q_m=\sum_h Q(h)L_m(roles_h),\qquad
 Q_m(h)=Q(h)L_m(roles_h)/q_m.
\]

These are the existing report_actions/random_report_outcomes and project_private operations: relative within-role acquisition histories survive the role projection. Only positive-q_m families are generated. No medium opportunities means one identity family.

Given Q_m, let f(h) be the living fake-seer claimant set, tau(h) the deterministic COMPLETE newly publishable truthful vector, and beta an approved fake plan. For zero fake claimants the plan is empty with probability1. More than one fake seer remains undefined. For one fake p, B_p(beta) is the current fake_backlog_options product law: every unpublished slot uses the same own PUBLIC past, original nonself target domain, unvisited-first fallback and uniform colors. This is not an invented sequential updating rule for unpublished fake slots.

For the configured noncontradictory/uniform policy, let T_p(Q_m) be the SET of distinct pending own report signatures in positive-Q_m hypotheses where p is true. If p has no possible true world, keep the full old noise law. Otherwise

\[
 A_p=B_p\cap T_p(Q_m),\qquad
 Z_p(Q_m)=\sum_{\beta\in A_p}B_p(\beta),\qquad
 F_p(\beta;Q_m)=B_p(\beta)1_{\beta\in A_p}/Z_p(Q_m).
\]

Here intersection means plan support, not multiplication of measures. Z=0 is the existing no_noncontradictory_fake_report refusal. Duplicate true signatures and their public probabilities do NOT multiply fake weights: plausibility is existence, not imitation of the true seer's posterior distribution. For the separately configured plain uniform policy, A is the whole base support and Z=1. A shortcut must retain the selected policy rather than silently switching between them.
