This extends ea24875 without changing Rules, configured strategies, Model-A Q, within-role secret mixtures, or physical mass. Publication is held. HELP through d86b93c is preserved.

## Reuse of complete data

CandidateRules compiles each exact information/action orbit's row replacements and masks once. Every original full input remains in the rule proof; no marginal-only symmetry establishes equality. Nonterminal scalar keys include the entire candidate map, full public and owned laws, history, target and configuration under the same ID bijection. A conservative size limit retains complete identity for larger inputs.

ID serialization has a bounded constructor-local memo of complete immutable Boards and states. Identity entries hold strong references; an object address cannot be reused for a different source. Cached role totals are derived from each immutable complete TrueScanState, retaining all role correlations. Shared acquisition generation retains every record and exact mass; its cache has 16 entries and never stores failed generation. This is distinct from profile-dependent values and policy selection.

## Exact terminal support partition

At the established boundary, every possible next execution physically ends the game and no public ability report is active. Public terminal choice depends on the positive supported full role worlds and their original relative Q weights. Private terminal payoff depends on the separate physical measure. Original acquisition, guard and bite histories are retained by callers.

For every candidate rule, compile the OR of generated role support contributed by each complete owned policy row. A dynamic program includes EVERY local nonempty uniform subset, storing multiplicity and one full witness for each joint support signature. All profiles in a signature have the same complete legal score vectors. Such a class can have at most one admissible profile: the uniform best response to that vector. It is accepted only if it maps back into the same class.

`proof=support_partition` counts the original profiles classified, including multiplicities; it does not claim to execute hundreds of millions of scalar evaluations. The real 387420489-profile fixture was classified uniquely in about 0.51 seconds. Every selected legal score matched the unreduced common action flow. Small exhaustive comparisons also cover unique, multiple and no-admissible outcomes, as well as a best response different from its class witness.

The terminal payoff table calls the existing physical action kernel, combining Q BEFORE public max selection. The physical measure uses the existing guard/night resolver. At this scalar-only boundary, a next unobserved scan kernel sums to one and has no later observation or strategic effect; its expansion can be integrated without constructing a shortened joint state. Existing scan records are never removed. History/replay and observer calls keep their full generation path. A direct comparison with full scan expansion checks exact physical role masses, both guard rules, living/dead prior guarded targets and a living hidden seer.

## Dependency equations and roster observation

A candidate replaces some full information rows. Its complete score cannot depend on the overwritten parameters. The generic solver records these dependencies, processes strongly connected components after their dependencies, and uniquely forces an independent block from each remaining assignment. Every outside assignment is included; responses can be asymmetric. Necessary best-response equations refute the other assignments without choosing an uncalculated value.

The implemented roster dependency reduction is bounded to FOUR survivors, fixed uniform public day actions, uniform guards, and no future public seer/medium reports. Within that boundary, owned wolf values are independent of OTHER initial wolf rosters' kernel rows. Larger sources keep their complete cross-roster dependencies unless the original candidate rule overwrites them. Generated secret normalization is per complete role world; global Q normalization cancels when conditioning on the wolf's own initial roster. Same-roster owned action histories remain distinct and coupled.

For four survivors, the internal scalar evaluator may condition the complete public source on that roster observation. It keeps ALL same-roster histories, guard records and scan records. The current candidate map is restricted from the ORIGINAL full-public rule map. The only subsequent night has three survivors and a fixed public-day continuation, whose values are independent of candidate grouping. Public UI, saved Boards, actual full kernels and history replay are never replaced by the conditional Board. Private seer/guard observers are excluded from this cut. Unreduced full-public action-flow comparisons with a living hidden seer, a medium and both guard rules check the decomposition.

Partial or ambiguous policy certificates remain Pending. All required legal rows of any retained candidate are computed before a unique complete policy is adopted. Work/time limits are unchanged. `proof=dependency_elimination` reports the original profile domain classified, not the number of feedback assignments actually evaluated.
